TIME OF DAY

Day and night distributions differ

The source analysis identified a heavier long-delay tail during daytime periods. Exact day/night counts were not preserved in the summarized extract used for this site, so no numeric shift comparison is invented here.

Daytime pattern

INTERPRETATION GUARDRAILS

What the data shows

  • Variation in RTP-to-assignment time
  • 53 cases assigned within 60 minutes
  • 29 cases exceeding 120 minutes
  • Higher mean census in the delayed cohort
  • 13 prolonged cases at census ≤16
  • A qualitative day/night distribution difference

LIMITS

What it does not independently show

  • The specific reason for every delay
  • Whether a staffing decision was appropriate
  • Whether discharge barriers caused an individual case
  • Departmental or role responsibility
  • Causality between census and turnaround time
  • Exact staffed / compatible bed capacity at every RTP event
